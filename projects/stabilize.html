Reclaim Creative — Stabilize

An assistive drawing system that filters essential tremor in real time, translating a shaking hand's intent into precise robotic brushstrokes.

Visit Project Site → ← Back to Projects

The Problem

Essential tremor affects over 10 million Americans, causing rhythmic, involuntary hand shaking that makes fine motor tasks — like drawing — nearly impossible. For artists, this means losing the primary means of self-expression.

Existing medical treatments are invasive or imprecise. Reclaim Creative takes a different approach: rather than suppressing the tremor in the body, filter it out in software and let a robot do the drawing.

How It Works

The system has three stages: sense the hand's position, filter out the tremor, and reproduce the intended motion with a precision plotter.

01

Sensing

Two VL53L7CX time-of-flight sensors with SPAD arrays triangulate hand position in 2D using the law of cosines at 60 Hz.

02

Filtering

A Nucleo-L476RG runs an IIR low-pass filter that removes tremor frequencies (5–12 Hz) while preserving intentional artistic motion.

03

Plotting

Filtered (x, y) coordinates stream over UART at 115,200 baud to a Simulink motor controller driving a custom Cartesian plotter.

By the Numbers

60 Hz Sensor sampling rate
5–12 Hz Tremor band filtered
80 steps/mm Plotter resolution
3 Isolated microcontrollers

Hardware Design

The plotter uses aluminum V-slot extrusion for the frame with NEMA 17 stepper motors driven by TMC2209 drivers (overcurrent protection included). A spring-loaded, servo-actuated pen lifter prevents surface damage when repositioning between strokes.
